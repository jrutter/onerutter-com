Storefront Next is Salesforce's new way to build B2C Commerce storefronts: a React app that runs on Managed Runtime and talks to Salesforce through the Commerce API, instead of the older SFRA templates and cartridges. More on how it works in the [Storefront Next template repo](https://github.com/SalesforceCommerceCloud/storefront-next-template).

## Why it matters to me

It's where commerce on Salesforce is heading, and I want to understand it properly, not just at slide level.

## Learning in short bursts

So I set up a Grok Bot agent as a Storefront Next tutor. Every weekday morning it sends me a ten-minute lesson pulled straight from the real repo, with a quick quiz. I'm 16 lessons in: routing, loading data from the Commerce API, auth and sessions, the cart, checkout, Page Designer, extensions, multi-site, and SEO. When something doesn't click, I just ask, like how it still talks to Business Manager, or whether you still need cartridges (mostly no).

## Then building something: Neon Coast

Reading only gets you so far, so I'm building Neon Coast, a fun adventure in throwback design and newer tech.
