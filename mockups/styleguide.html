Style guide

Paper, Thames green, and clay. Outfit for the interface. Source Serif 4 when a note is long enough to read. This page is not part of the public site.

Color

Warm paper, like the reference, with a river green and a clay accent instead of that site's coral and orange. Text on paper uses Ink or Ink soft. Clay is for kickers, the current-page mark, and the dashed “needs Jake” boxes.

  • Paper#F3EEE6Page background
  • Paper deep#E7E0D4Bands, code chips
  • Cream#FBF7F1Text on dark cards
  • Ink#1B1714Headlines, body
  • Ink soft#3F3A36Secondary text
  • Muted#5C564EMeta, captions
  • Thames#1E4A42Links in notes, live pill
  • Thames deep#14352FGradient start
  • Clay#C45C32Kickers, focus
  • Clay deep#8E3B22Hello link, labels
  • Gold#E2A15AKicker on dark cards
  • Gold soft#F6E2C4Draft banner

Type scale

Outfit throughout the chrome, in the same job Be Vietnam Pro does on the reference: a large geometric sans, tight leading, slight negative tracking. Source Serif 4 is reserved for the body of a note.

Note body · Source Serif 4

A note should feel like writing, not another marketing block. Sixty-six characters is the measure. Drafts wear a gold banner so a placeholder cannot be mistaken for Jake.

Spacing

An 8-point scale: 8, 12, 16, 20, 28, 32, 36, 48, 56, 64, 80, 96. Content column is 1120px. Side gutters are 32px, 20px on a phone. Sections stack with 96px between them, 56px on a phone. The header is 76px, sticky, with a light blur. Story text stays under 68 characters. A note stays under 66.

Components

Text links, not filled buttons. Status is a small pill. Anything Jake still has to write sits in a dashed clay box, including on the finished pages.

Motion

  • Featured cards lift 3px and deepen the shadow. 200ms, ease.
  • Text links grow a clay underline on hover. No color jump.
  • The header stays put and blurs the paper behind it. It does not hide on scroll.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce removes the lift and any future fade.
  • Left out on purpose: particle field, pulsing notification dot, theme crossfade. Those are on the reference and not in this version.
